# Community expectations

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Discuss ideas and evidence without harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of private information. Give newcomers room to learn and offer actionable feedback.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and project spaces. Report concerns privately to maintainers when possible. Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation, with proportionate decisions that protect the community.
